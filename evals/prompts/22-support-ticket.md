tags: a11y

Build a "raise a support request" form: a topic chosen from a list, a subject, a description with a character limit of 500 that shows how many are left, an optional attachment, and how the person wants to be contacted (email, phone or no preference). After submitting, show a confirmation with the request's reference number.
