Build a delivery address form: full name, phone, emirate (a list to choose from), area, building, flat number, and delivery notes. Name, phone, emirate and area are required. In the mock state the person has submitted with the phone number missing and an invalid flat number, so those errors show. This app ships in Arabic as well as English; write the copy in English.
