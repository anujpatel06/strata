Build the second step of a three-step insurance claim form: uploading evidence. Show which step the person is on. They can add up to five photos or PDFs, see each file with its size, and remove one. One file in the mock data failed to upload and shows why. Include Back and Continue actions. Continue is unavailable until at least one file has uploaded.
