Build a checkout summary for a grocery order. Show the delivery address with a Change link, the delivery slot as a choice between three times, the items with a quantity stepper each, a promo code field, and the price breakdown: subtotal, delivery, discount and total. End with a "Place order" action. This app ships in Arabic as well as English; write the copy in English.
